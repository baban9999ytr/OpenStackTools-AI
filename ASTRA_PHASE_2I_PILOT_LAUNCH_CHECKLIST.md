# ASTRA Phase 2I pilot launch checklist

The pilot is website-only and supervised during the business's agreed working hours. Use synthetic records in the acceptance walkthrough. Each sign-off below requires the named role and the date, candidate revision, evidence link and decision.

## Business employee walkthrough

- [ ] Sign in with your own verified staff account, select the test organization and verify another business is inaccessible.
- [ ] Create and edit a property. Enter its verified price and area, set a zero maintenance fee, add or correct the deposit, and leave an unknown fact empty. Refresh and verify the information. Mark the property sold and check that the assistant stops recommending it.
- [ ] Use the test widget as a visitor to create an inquiry. In **Conversations**, select the thread, refresh and inspect property preferences. Take over, reply as staff and check the customer sees it. Verify a subsequent message remains with staff.
- [ ] In **Receptionist**, have an authorized manager configure local business hours and enable a consultant for property viewings. Set working windows and verify both an available time and a blocked time.
- [ ] Choose a verified time, obtain a separate confirmation and complete the test booking. Find it in Receptionist and verify the property, consultant, local start and status. Work through a customer-approved change and cancellation. Reconfirm any changed time.
- [ ] When property information is unavailable, consult the current record or say it is unconfirmed. On a synthetic provider failure, identify the dashboard category, take over and escalate with the timestamp and request correlation ID. Respect a customer's refusal to share a phone number.

- [ ] Data owner approves collection purpose, consent and refusal handling, authorized roles, retention, export and deletion workflow, incident contacts and backup custody.
- [ ] Business owner obtains legal review for notices, communications, data handling and other obligations where required. Technical tests provide evidence of behavior.

## Operator release gates

- [ ] Pin source and image digests, verify actual HTTPS ingress, HSTS, proxy trust, security settings, account email, widget origin and secret separation. Keep external channels disabled.
- [ ] Approve one current Luna verification, confirm project configuration and budgets, and complete the independent Turkish review with no blocking defects.
- [ ] Demonstrate monitored readiness, AI and queue failures, budget exhaustion and stale handoffs on the intended release stack. Confirm a real on-call owner receives and acknowledges a test alert.
- [ ] Establish backup and restore access, encrypted off-host retention, recovery point and time objectives, and rollback compatibility. Verify the intended host and deployment configuration.
- [ ] Cap scope and agree stop conditions. Pause automated replies for an unauthorized or inconsistent booking, tenant disclosure, staff coverage loss, sustained outage or unresolved cost anomaly. Continue staffed intake only under the approved workflow.
